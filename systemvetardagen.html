Systemvetar­dagen

In 2022 I worked in the design team for Systemvetardagen. The task was to design a website and a mobile application for the anual fair at Stockholm University. My role was to iteratively design two different prototypes using figma.

Design Process

The brandguidlines seen below illustrate some of the rules I had to take into account when designing. I attempted to produce design that both aligned with the guidelines while adding my own touch to the project. It resulted in a responsive website that presents information about the fair in a clean yet playful way. My co-designer and I iterated through several prototypes before finalizing the last versions.

My Role

Designed two prototypes for the landing page of Systemvetardagen — the annual fair held at Stockholm University. Iterated on multiple prototypes before deciding on the final version. Created a design that aligned with the brand guidelines.

Two Figma prototypes — one for mobile and one for desktop.
