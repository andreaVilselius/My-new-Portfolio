Birthday notifications

My first feature shipped to the Wndy codebase, three weeks into my internship. A small detail that makes sure no employee is left out of birthday reminders.

Frontend UX/UI Forms
Problem

Adding a personnummer is optional in Wndy. But employees who chose not to share it missed out: their managers never got a reminder before their birthday.

Solution
  • If a personnummer is entered, the birthday is read from it automatically.
  • If not, the employee can enter just their date of birth.
  • A hint under the field shows exactly when the reminder will be sent.
Result

Admins and managers linked to the employee now get an email five days before the birthday, whichever option the employee chooses.
